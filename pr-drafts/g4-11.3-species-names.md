# PR draft: pr/g4-11.3-species-names

Branch: `pr/g4-11.3-species-names` (one commit on top of `main` 4c95802)
Files: `scorers/dnaDamage/TsScoreDNADamageSBS.cc`, `scorers/dnaDamage/TsScoreDNADamageSBS.hh` (+42/-1)

## Title

Restore hydroxyl damage on Geant4 11.3.x, which renamed the radical species

## Body

### Defect

Geant4 11.3 renamed several radical species by prefixing a degree sign, so the names reaching `TsScoreDNADamageSBS::ProcessHits` through `GetMolecule()->GetName()` became `°OH^0`, `°OH^-1` and `°O^0`. Every test in the indirect-damage branch of that method is a raw string equality against the old spelling (`OH^0`), so `isHydroxil` and `isSpeciesToKill` stopped matching on 11.3 and all hydroxyl-mediated damage disappeared. Nothing warned: no exception, no message, only far less damage. Since backbone damage on this path is hydroxyl-only whereas bases can also be damaged by the solvated electron, whose name `e_aq^-1` did not change, the signature is a large loss of strand breaks with a smaller loss of base damage. `TsIRTConfiguration` already carries this translation in `fGeant4NameOverrides` for the IRT path; the step-by-step scorer had no equivalent.

### Symptom, measured

Setup: `examples/scorers/SBSDamageToDNANucleus/ExampleNucleusDNADamage.txt` unchanged apart from the history count (5 MeV protons through the sphere nucleus model, g4em-dna + TsEmDNAChemistry, step-by-step chemistry), two protons per run, three seeds, one thread, on Geant4 11.3.2. Counts are read from the scorer's `DNADamage` ntuple, which the example writes broken down by damage origin.

Per-event rows of the `DNADamage` ntuple (energy imparted in keV; SBs and BDs are the total strand breaks and base damages, the `_Indirect` columns the hydroxyl- and electron-mediated part). Seed 1 produced one scored event, seeds 2 and 3 two each. The energy per event is identical between the two builds, as it should be, since the patch touches only the chemistry bookkeeping.

| 1, 0 | 36.8 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

Before the fix, the scorer recorded zero indirect strand breaks in every event, while indirect base damage stayed present (75 in total), which is the electron-only signature described above. After the fix, 48 indirect strand breaks appeared over the five events, total strand breaks rose 3.4x (20 to 68) and base damage 1.5x (93 to 139). The one seed whose two builds agreed to every digit (seed 1) carried a single 36.8 keV event with two lesions, so no hydroxyl reached a DNA volume there.

On identical decks and seeds, the earlier measurement that motivated this fix (Geant4 11.2.2 / G4EMLOW 8.5 against 11.3.2 / G4EMLOW 8.6.1, six tracks) gave 97 strand breaks and 219 base damages on 11.2.2 against 19 and 131 on 11.3.2: strand breaks fell 5.1x while base damage fell 1.7x. Radical production itself did not change, since G-values from `examples/scorers/SBSGetGValue` agreed to four decimal places across the two releases, so the loss sat entirely in this scorer's name matching.

### Fix

Add a static `NormalizeSpeciesName` to the scorer that maps the 11.3 spellings back to the ones the comparisons expect (`°OH^0` to `OH^0`, `°OH^-1` to `OH^-1`, `°O^0` to `O^0`, `O°^-1` to `O^-1`, `H_O2°^0` to `HO2^0`, and the `O_2`/`O_3` forms), mirroring `TsIRTConfiguration::fGeant4NameOverrides`, and apply it once at the top of the indirect-damage branch. Names that are not in the table pass through untouched, so the scorer behaves identically on releases that still use the old spelling. Two files, +42/-1.

## Discussion reply draft (#125 / #126)

The behavior you describe comes from a species-name change in Geant4 11.3: the radicals now carry a degree-sign prefix (`°OH^0` instead of `OH^0`), and `TsScoreDNADamageSBS` compared the name with a plain string equality, so on 11.3 the hydroxyl branch never matched and no indirect strand break could be scored, whatever the scavenging and damage probabilities were set to. Base damage survived in part because the solvated electron kept its name. PR [number] adds the name translation the IRT path already had, and on the shipped nucleus example the indirect strand breaks returned (numbers in the PR). Until it is merged, building against Geant4 11.2.x restores the old counts.
